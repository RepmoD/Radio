# Ballgame Radio

A vintage table-radio web app for old baseball radio broadcasts, made for a phone home screen.

- AM band: games streamed straight from public Internet Archive collections (mostly 1934 to 1974), plus a live search of other archive.org uploads.
- FM band: full-game radio playlists on YouTube from the Retro Sports Radio channel.
- Year dial, search, resume where you stopped, starred games, favorite announcers, Today (games played on this date), Rain delay (a vintage commercial break), and play a season's games in order.

Nothing is rehosted here: audio streams from where it is already published. Your positions and favorites stay in the browser on your device.
